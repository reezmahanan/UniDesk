APEX UNIVERSITY - MAHAPOLA HIGHER EDUCATION SCHOLARSHIP AND BURSARY POLICY (YEAR 2026)

1. Eligibility Criteria:
The Mahapola Higher Education Scholarship is awarded annually based on the University Grants Commission (UGC) merit and parental income criteria. Students with parental annual income below LKR 500,000 are eligible for Ordinary Bursaries, while top Z-Score holders in each district receive Merit Scholarships.

2. Payout and Disbursement:
Disbursements are made monthly at the rate of LKR 5,000 per installment. Payment vouchers are issued at the Student Welfare Division starting from the 10th of every month. Students must physically present their valid Student Record Book and National Identity Card (NIC). Vouchers can be cashed at the Bank of Ceylon (BOC) Campus Branch or credited directly to linked student accounts.

3. Attendance Requirements:
Students must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance in lectures and practical sessions. Any suspension or academic disciplinary action will temporarily freeze stipend installments.
