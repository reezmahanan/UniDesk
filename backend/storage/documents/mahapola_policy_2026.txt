APEX UNIVERSITY - MAHAPOLA HIGHER EDUCATION SCHOLARSHIP AND BURSARY POLICY (YEAR 2026)

1. Eligibility Criteria:
The Mahapola Higher Education Scholarship is awarded annually based on the University Grants Commission (UGC) merit and parental income criteria. Students with parental annual income below LKR 500,000 are eligible for Ordinary Bursaries, while top Z-Score holders in each district receive Merit Scholarships. Students must be enrolled as full-time undergraduates in a government university or higher educational institution recognised by the UGC.

2. How to Collect Mahapola Voucher - Step by Step:
Step 1: Visit the Student Welfare Division (Admin Block, Ground Floor, Room 005) between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM on weekdays.
Step 2: Bring your valid Student Record Book (Blue Book) and your National Identity Card (NIC).
Step 3: Sign the disbursement register maintained by the Welfare Officer.
Step 4: Collect your payment voucher. Vouchers are issued from the 10th of each month.
Step 5: Take the voucher to the Bank of Ceylon (BOC) Campus Branch to encash it. Alternatively, students who have linked their bank account to the student portal will receive a direct credit transfer.
If you missed the primary collection window (10th-25th), alternate collection dates are the last two working days of the month. Students who miss two consecutive months must re-verify their eligibility at the Welfare Division.

3. Payout and Disbursement:
Disbursements are made monthly at the rate of LKR 5,000 per installment for Ordinary Bursary holders. Merit Scholarship recipients receive LKR 7,500 per month. Payment vouchers are issued at the Student Welfare Division starting from the 10th of every month. Students must physically present their valid Student Record Book and National Identity Card (NIC). Vouchers can be cashed at the Bank of Ceylon (BOC) Campus Branch or credited directly to linked student accounts.

4. Attendance Requirements:
Students must maintain a minimum of 80% attendance in lectures and practical sessions to remain eligible. Any suspension or academic disciplinary action will temporarily freeze stipend installments. Students on approved medical leave are exempt for the duration certified by the university medical officer.

5. Renewal and Continuation:
The scholarship is renewed annually subject to satisfactory academic performance and continued income eligibility. Students must resubmit income verification documents (Grama Niladhari Certificate or Tax Declaration) each July at the Welfare Division. Failure to resubmit within the deadline will result in suspension of the following semester's payments.
