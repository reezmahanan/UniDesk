APEX UNIVERSITY - EXAMINATIONS, RESULTS AND ACADEMIC AFFAIRS GUIDE (2026)

1. Semester Examination Registration:
All students must register for semester examinations through the Student Portal (portal.apex.edu.lk) before the registration deadline, typically 3 weeks before the exam date. Late registration incurs a fine of LKR 500 per module. Students with outstanding fees or library fines will have their exam registration automatically blocked.

2. How to Check Examination Results:
Step 1: Log in to the Student Portal at portal.apex.edu.lk using your index number and password.
Step 2: Navigate to "My Results" under the Academics section.
Step 3: Select the relevant semester and year to view your module results.
Step 4: Results are published within 6 weeks after the examination date. SMS notifications are sent to the registered mobile number when results are released.

3. Result Appeals and Re-Corrections:
If you believe an error occurred in marking, you may apply for a re-correction within 14 days of the result publication. Submit the Re-Correction Request Form (available at Examinations Division, Admin Block Room 102) along with the payment of LKR 1,500 per module. Results of re-corrections are final and binding.

4. Supplementary and Re-Sit Examinations:
Students who fail a module are eligible to sit the supplementary examination held at the beginning of the following semester. Register through the Student Portal or at the Examinations Division. The re-sit fee is LKR 2,000 per module.

5. Medical Leave and Exam Deferments:
Students who are unable to sit an examination due to a medical emergency must submit a Medical Certificate issued by a registered government physician within 5 working days of the examination to the Examinations Division. The Dean of the Faculty must approve the deferment. Deferred examinations will be scheduled within the same academic year.

6. Transcript and Degree Certificate Requests:
Official transcripts can be requested at the Examinations Division or through the Student Portal. Processing takes 5 to 10 working days. Fee: LKR 1,000 per copy. Degree certificates are awarded at the annual graduation ceremony.
