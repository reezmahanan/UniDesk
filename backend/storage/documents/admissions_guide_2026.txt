APEX UNIVERSITY - ADMISSIONS, REGISTRATION AND STUDENT ID GUIDE (2026)

1. New Student Registration:
New students admitted via UGC must complete registration at the Admissions Division within the first two weeks of the academic year. Required documents: Original GCE A/L Certificate, NIC, Birth Certificate, two passport-size photos, Grama Niladhari Certificate. Registration fee: LKR 5,000 (payable at the university cashier or via student portal online payment).

2. Student Identity Card (ID Card):
Student ID cards are issued during the first week of registration. Lost or damaged ID cards can be replaced at the Admissions Division (Admin Block Room 010) for a fee of LKR 500. Present your registration receipt and a passport-size photo. Processing takes 3 working days.

3. GCE A/L Minimum Requirements by Faculty:
- Faculty of Computing: Three passes (at least one 'B') in Physical Science stream (Combined Mathematics, Physics or Chemistry or ICT).
- Faculty of Engineering: Three 'B' passes in Combined Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.
- Faculty of Business: Three 'S' passes in Commerce or Physical Science stream. English medium.
- Faculty of Medicine: Three 'A' passes in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.
All admissions are strictly based on UGC district quota and Z-Score ranking.

4. Deferment of Admission:
Students offered admission who cannot join in the current year must apply for deferment within 2 weeks of receiving the UGC admission letter. Deferment is granted for one year only. Submit the Deferment Request Form to the Admissions Division.

5. Inter-Faculty Transfer:
Students may apply for a transfer between faculties during the first semester only, subject to availability of places and minimum Z-Score requirements of the target faculty. Applications must be submitted with Dean's approval from both faculties.
